# 🎭 Dialogue Comedy Shorts Production Guide (Dr. Ethan Kwan Format)

## Overview
This document outlines the mechanics and best practices for generating viral dual-voiceover comedy shorts with Autoposter.

---

## 1. The Comedic Pacing Formula

1. **Speech Rate**: Set to `+15%` to `+18%` above standard TTS rates (~240-250 WPM) to keep audience engagement high and drop-off low.
2. **Turn-Taking Silence**: Between speaker turns, add exactly `0.18s` to `0.22s` of silence. Never exceed 0.3s of pause between lines.
3. **Character Asymmetry**:
   - **Voice A (The Delusional Instigator)**: High energy, defensive, fast-talking (`en-US-ChristopherNeural` or `am_michael`).
   - **Voice B (The Straight Man / Troll)**: Dry, deadpan, cynical (`en-US-GuyNeural` or `am_adam`).

---

## 2. Audio & Video Balancing

- **Visuals**: Vertical 1080x1920 (9:16) 60FPS Minecraft parkour or clean high-speed gameplay footage.
- **Audio Mix**:
  - Voiceover tracks normalized to `1.0` volume.
  - Background music ducked to `0.12 - 0.14` volume.
- **Subtitles**: Keep subtitles **off** by default to maintain the raw cinematic storytelling aesthetic.
